routing validation, and other earlier SI analyses are not regenerated by the 17

some rurality shares differ after geographic reconciliation.

## Historical figures

`results/nature_health_v137/figures/` is retained byte-for-byte from the public
original commit. Figure 1 contains legends over southeastern geography. Figure 3
has a blue/green legend mismatch and obscured Florida in one panel. The original
Figure 2 is a travel-time distribution, whereas v143 Figure 2 is a rurality/income
comparison. These defects and numbering changes are disclosed, not hidden by
moving the original tag. Revised figures prepared locally use complete geography
and external keys and do not repeat the unsupported survey-noise explanation in
image text. They are not yet included in the public revision snapshot.
